PROTOTYPE MODE – IMPORTANT This is a demonstration prototype. It does not contact counselors, police, emergency services, or child-protection agencies. Do not enter real names, passwords, addresses, phone numbers, images, or detailed information about abuse.

Welcome to SafeVoice

SafeVoice is a direct youth-safety support tool. It helps children and teenagers take a clear, low-barrier first step toward verified help when they feel unable to speak to a parent or guardian immediately.

This prototype does not collect, store, or send real reports. A full deployment would require verified counselors, child-protection partners, trained moderators, legal review, security testing, privacy safeguards, and verified local contacts.

I Need Help Now

This prototype cannot contact emergency services automatically.

If someone is in immediate danger:

  • Go to a safer place if you can do so safely.
  • Tell a trusted adult immediately.
  • Contact the appropriate local emergency or child-protection service.
  • Do not confront an unsafe person alone.
  • If online blackmail is involved, do not send more money or images.

A real app must verify local emergency and child-protection contacts before displaying them.

Talk to a Trained Supporter

PROTOTYPE MODE This does not connect you to counselors or emergency services. Do not enter real personal information.

A full SafeVoice service would connect users only with:

  • Verified child-safety counselors.
  • Trained mental-health professionals.
  • Authorized child-protection professionals.
  • Approved cyber-safety specialists for online threats.

It must never connect a child directly with an unknown adult. It must never allow unmoderated private messaging.

Report Something Safely

PROTOTYPE MODE This does NOT submit reports. Do not enter real personal information.

A full reporting system should:

  • Explain what information will be collected.
  • Collect only essential information.
  • Explain who will receive the report.
  • Encrypt data during transfer and storage.
  • Send urgent situations to trained safeguarding staff.
  • Show immediate safety guidance.

This prototype does not collect, save, or send any reports.

Safety and Rights

Everyone deserves to be treated safely and respectfully. Abuse, threats, bullying, blackmail, and exploitation are not your fault. If it is safe, consider telling a trusted adult, teacher, counselor, doctor, relative, or child-protection professional.

Online Threats and Blackmail

If someone pressures, threatens, or blackmails you online, do not send more images, money, passwords, or personal information. Do not meet the person alone. If it is safe, save usernames, dates, and messages, then ask a trusted adult or verified cyber-safety service for help.

Bullying

Bullying is not your fault. If it is safe, save dates, messages, or screenshots. Tell a trusted adult or use available reporting processes. Avoid responding to threats alone.

A Simple Safety Plan

Think of one trusted adult and one backup adult you could contact. If you feel unsafe, move toward a safer public place when possible. Do not confront a person who may harm you, and do not tell an unsafe person about your plan.

Getting Support

You deserve support from trained people. A counselor can provide emotional support. A child-protection professional can help with safeguarding. A cyber-safety specialist can help with online threats. This prototype cannot replace these services.

Community Support, Moderated

PROTOTYPE MODE This does not run a real community. Do not enter real personal information.

Peer encouragement can reduce loneliness, but it must be safe.

A full community-support feature would require:

  • Anonymous usernames instead of real names.
  • No private messaging.
  • No adult pretending to be a peer.
  • No sharing addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or images.
  • Trained moderators reviewing content.
  • Clear reporting tools and safety rules.
  • Escalation of high-risk posts to trained professionals.

Example supportive messages:

  • [Moderator] "You are not alone."
  • [Moderator] "What happened is not your fault."
  • [Moderator] "Please contact a trusted adult or trained helper."

About SafeVoice

SafeVoice — Youth Safety Support Prototype (2026)

SafeVoice is a direct youth-safety support and reporting concept. It gives children and teenagers a clear, low-barrier first step toward verified help when they feel unable to tell a parent or guardian immediately.

The concept includes:

  • Urgent-help guidance.
  • Verified professional-support pathways.
  • Safer reporting pathways.
  • Age-appropriate safety resources.
  • Moderated community-support principles.

This is a demonstration prototype only. It is not a real counseling, emergency, police, or child-protection service. Do not use this in a real crisis. For demonstration purposes only.
